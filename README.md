In June 2026 I ran identical pink noise through all 24 channels of the desk at unity, with my own capture process. Twenty came back alive, and each one is a VOICE in BR108: its own measured colour, its own drive character, its own place on the desk. **COLOUR** scales that channel's personality from flat to four times what was measured, **DRIVE** is its saturation, biased by how dirty the channel really got, **WARMTH** is the shared desk colour on top. Then the console EQ (LOW, MID, HIGH with switched frequencies, plus HPF and LPF), a printed-scale fader and a VU meter per side, LINK and SWAP for stereo, thirty-four volume-matched factory presets and community presets loaded from inside the plugin.
